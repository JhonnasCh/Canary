THE PROBLEM

AI-generated apps are born vulnerable

AI writes functional code, but rarely secure code. These are the most common vulnerabilities we find.

87%

Missing Security Headers

Missing Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options and other critical protection headers.

73%

Insecure Cookies

Session cookies without HttpOnly, Secure or SameSite flags properly configured.

64%

Outdated Dependencies

Frameworks and libraries with known vulnerabilities (CVEs) left unpatched.

91%

No Bot Protection

No rate limiting, no honeypots, no automated behavior detection in place.
